## Technical Insights & Architecture

This component is constructed using a 3-layer sandwich visual architecture:

1. **Layer 1 (Background SVGs)**: The open lid flaps, inside back wall, and interior shadow folds of the carton box.
2. **Layer 2 (Middle Motion Stage)**: The animated floating chip managed by `AnimatePresence mode="popLayout"`, starting tucked inside the box cavity, springing upward, and floating away.
3. **Layer 3 (Foreground SVGs)**: The front carton body and dropped lip flap with `pointer-events-none`, naturally occluding the lower part of the chip as it emerges from inside the package.
